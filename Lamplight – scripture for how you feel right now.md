Lamplight offers a reflective prompt based on rough patterns in heart rate and voice. It is not a medical or diagnostic tool and can be wrong. Audio is analysed on this device and is never recorded or uploaded. If you are in distress or thinking of harming yourself, please contact a local emergency number or a crisis line.
